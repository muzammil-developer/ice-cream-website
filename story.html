Our Story

Eleven years of small-batch churning, in a shop that still smells like fresh waffle cones.

Home/Story
Where it began

A 12-seat shop with one machine.

In 2014 we signed a lease on a tiny storefront in Williamsburg with two friends, a hand-me-down batch freezer, and a chalkboard menu that changed every morning.

The idea was simple: use the best ingredients we could find, churn only what we could sell that day, and never compromise on texture. Eleven years later, that's still the plan.

Today we run three shops across Brooklyn, but every flavor still starts with the same question: what's ripe this week?

What We Believe

Three things we won't compromise on.

Real Ingredients

No artificial colors, flavors, or stabilizers. If we can't pronounce it, it doesn't go in the batch.

Small Batches

We churn every flavor the morning it's sold. Nothing sits in the freezer longer than a day.

Local First

Dairy from upstate New York, produce from the Union Square greenmarket, everything else as close to home as possible.

How We Make It

Slow churned, never rushed.

A small batch takes us three days from start to scoop. Here's what happens in between.

01

Source

Every morning we visit our dairy and produce partners in upstate New York. What's ripe today determines tomorrow's menu.

02

Infuse

Bases rest for 24 hours so the flavors fully develop. Pistachios are stone-ground; vanilla beans are split by hand.

03

Churn

Small batches are churned to order, packed into pint containers, and served the same afternoon. Always fresh.
