Our Story

Started with one machine and a stubborn idea.

In 2014 we opened a 12-seat shop in Brooklyn with one batch freezer and a chalkboard menu. The idea was simple: use the best ingredients we could find, and never make more than we could sell that day.

Eleven years later, we still churn every flavor the morning it's sold. Nothing sits. Nothing is frozen twice.
